# soul-fruits
use the soul or lightningXghost to defend urself. the game is powered by HTML

the skills of soul fruit. (i will add lightningXghost soon)
*fruits*
soul fruit
passive: spawns 2 spirit souls (left side is the sun and right side is the dark purple cloud. they follow player), get 5% damage reduction buff and 1% lifesteal for all skills.
skill 1: soul snatch
the cloud rush to nearby enemies and make bursts of AoE that attack enemies and heal player health by 10% per DMG. CD: 4s
skill 2: fire breath
the sun moves to nearby enemies and blasts a continuous breath of fire that burn enemies (deals 6.6% DMG per tick) for about 5 seconds. range: 3 meters, breath of fire width: 2 meters, breath of fire length: 4 meters. CD: 6.5s
skill 3: thunder strike/thunder rain/thunder judgement
*thunder strike: the cloud moves up and expands itself to strike lightning on the ground. lightning explosion radius: 6 meters. CD: 3s, *thunder rain: the cloud moves up and expands itself to strike 11 lightning on the ground at random position. explosion radius: 6 meters, cast radius: 5 meters, strike interval: 0.27s. CD 3s, *thunder judgement: the cloud moves up and expands itself to strike an overlapping lightning that strikes in one spot where player aims for 2.5 seconds, strike radius: 6.5 meters, enemiesCanLift: true, lift speed: 4 meters per second. CD 3s
*can be switched by pressing "change to"*

skill 4: wrath of Ra
the sun blasts a powerful rising fire pillar that lifts enemies upward, dealing 7% burn DMG. pillar radius: 5.5 meters, duration: 1 second, burn duration: 4s, lift speed: 10 meters per second. CD: 6s
skill 5: end of life
the both sun and cloud fires a beam at mouse position for 3 seconds before exploding into large expanding AoE. sucks enemies before explosion. explosion radius: 4 meters, explosion expansion speed: 8 meters per second, enemy suck range: 2 meters, max range: 15 meters. CD: 9s
skill 6: soul flight
rides the cloud to flight to fly forward. fly speed: 25 meters per second, CD: 1s
